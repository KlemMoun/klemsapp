Puzzle Almanac is a daily puzzle game for iPhone. Four puzzles every day —
Lexicon, Sums, Links and Ladder — plus every day back to 1 January 2026, all
playable offline.

---

## There is no account, and nothing to recover

Puzzle Almanac has no server, no login and no cloud save. Your progress, your
streak and your settings are stored on your own device and nowhere else.

**This means: if you delete the app, your progress is gone permanently.** There
is no way for us to restore it, because we never had a copy. If your iPhone is
backed up to iCloud, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's
terms — restoring the phone from a backup restores the game with it.

---

## Common questions

**The app shows the wrong day.**
The day rolls over at midnight on your phone's own clock. If the date looks
wrong, check Settings → General → Date & Time on the iPhone.

**My streak reset.**
A day counts towards the streak when you solve at least one of its four puzzles
**on that day itself**. Solving an old day from the Archive is credited in your
statistics, but it does not extend a streak — otherwise the streak could be
manufactured in an afternoon and would stop meaning anything.

**I cannot find a puzzle I played before.**
Open the Archive from the Today screen. Every day back to 1 January 2026 is
there, in full.

**A word was rejected that I think is valid.**
Lexicon accepts a curated word list rather than a full dictionary. If a word you
would expect is missing, please tell us — the list is updated between releases.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. Never, for anything. The game works in aeroplane mode and will keep working
regardless of what happens to any server.

**Is there a subscription, or anything to buy inside the app?**
No. You bought the app once. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no timers
and nothing to unlock.

**Accessibility.**
Puzzle Almanac supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, and a colour-blind mode that
puts symbols on the tiles so that colour is never the only signal. The
colour-blind mode and a higher-contrast tile setting are both in Settings.
