**Four puzzles a day, in one small app you bought once.**

A five-letter word. A hidden sum. Sixteen words in four groups. A word ladder
climbed one letter at a time. Monday is gentle, Saturday is hard, and Sunday
lets up again — so a bad Saturday never ends the run.

No ads. No account. No timers, no lives, no energy, nothing to unlock and
nothing else to buy. No internet connection, ever: Puzzle Almanac works in
aeroplane mode, on the Underground, on a flight, and it will still work in ten
years when the servers other puzzle apps depend on have been switched off.

## The four puzzles

**Lexicon** — Find the five-letter word in six guesses. Tiles tell you what is
placed and what is merely present.

**Sums** — Find the hidden equation. Every guess has to be a true sum itself,
which is a different kind of thinking and the reason people keep this one for
last.

**Links** — Sixteen words belong to four groups of four. Some words look like
they belong to two. Four mistakes and the day is over.

**Ladder** — Climb from one word to another, changing a single letter at a time.
Any valid route counts, not just the one we had in mind.

## The archive

Every day back to 1 January 2026 is playable, in full, from the moment you
install. Nothing is drip-fed and nothing expires.

## Accessibility

Full VoiceOver labelling, Dynamic Type throughout, and a colour-blind mode that
puts symbols on the tiles so colour is never the only signal.
