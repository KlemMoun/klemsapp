**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

## The short version

Puzzle Almanac collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no server to send it to.

## What is stored

The app saves your puzzle progress, your streak and your two settings on your
own device, using the operating system's ordinary app storage. That is all it
saves.

This data never leaves the phone. There is no account, no login, no sign-up, and
no cloud backup performed by the app. If your phone is backed up by iCloud or by
Google, the app's data is included in that backup under those services' own
terms — that is a function of the operating system, not of this app.

## What is not collected

- No personal information. Not a name, an email address, a phone number or a
  location.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage measurement.
- No advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in this app.
- No third-party SDK that collects anything.

## Network access

The app makes no network requests. It works with the device in aeroplane mode
and behaves identically whether or not the phone has a connection.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app. Everything it stored goes with it. There is no copy anywhere
else.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at the same
address and the date above will change.
