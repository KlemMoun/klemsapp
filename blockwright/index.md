**200 block puzzles, every one of them provably solvable.**

Most block puzzles deal you random pieces and let you lose to a shape you were
never going to be given. Blockwright does the opposite. Every level in the
campaign was built by solving it backwards, so a solution using exactly the
pieces you are handed is guaranteed to exist — and the game proves it again on
every release rather than promising it.

When you get stuck, it is not the game's fault. Undo is unlimited and free, and
the app tells you the moment a position has stopped being winnable instead of
letting you place three more pieces and find out at the end.

No ads. No account. No timers, no lives, no energy, nothing to unlock and
nothing else to buy. No internet connection, ever: Blockwright works in
aeroplane mode, on the Underground, on a flight, and it will still work in ten
years when the servers other puzzle games depend on have been switched off.

## The campaign

200 levels across 8 chapters, each with a fixed, designed sequence of pieces
rather than a random draw. Chapters unlock as you go; difficulty climbs inside
each one and eases at the start of the next.

## Endless mode

The old random survival loop is in there too, for when you want it — and the app
says plainly on that screen that the winnable-every-time promise does not apply
there. It is a different game, and pretending otherwise would be the thing this
one was built to avoid.

## Languages

English, French and Spanish, switchable in Settings at any time. The game
follows your phone's language on first launch.

## Accessibility

VoiceOver throughout: every piece can be placed by tapping a piece and then
tapping a cell, so the whole game is playable without a drag. Dynamic Type is
supported, and dark and light themes follow your phone or can be set by hand.
