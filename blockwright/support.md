Blockwright is a block puzzle game for iPhone: 200 hand-built levels across 8
chapters, every one of them provably solvable, plus an Endless mode. It works
entirely offline.

---

## There is no account, and nothing to recover

Blockwright has no server, no login and no cloud save. Your completed levels,
your best Endless score and your settings are stored on your own device and
nowhere else.

**This means: if you delete the app, your progress is gone permanently.** There
is no way for us to restore it, because we never had a copy. If your iPhone is
backed up to iCloud, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's
terms — restoring the phone from a backup restores the game with it.

## Common questions

**I am stuck on a level. Is it actually solvable?**
Yes. Every campaign level was built by solving it backwards and is re-proved by a
solver on every release, so a solution using exactly the pieces you are given
always exists. Being stuck means this particular position has no way home — undo
and try a different placement. Undo is unlimited.

**The game says "No way home". What does that mean?**
That from the position you are in, the pieces you have left cannot clear the
board. It is not the end of the level: undo, and the level is winnable again from
an earlier move. The game tells you as soon as it is true rather than letting you
place three more pieces first.

**What is the difference between "No way home" and "Board not clear"?**
"No way home" means there are still pieces in hand but no winning line from here.
"Board not clear" means every piece has been placed and the board is not empty —
undo to get a piece back, or restart the level.

**Endless mode let me lose. I thought every level was winnable?**
The promise is about the campaign, and the Endless screen says so. Endless deals
random pieces on purpose, for players who want that loop; there is no guarantee
there and none is claimed.

**How do I change the language?**
Settings → Language. English, French and Spanish. The game follows your phone's
language the first time you open it.

**Can I play without dragging?**
Yes. Tap a piece to select it, then tap a cell on the grid. This is the path
VoiceOver uses, and it works the same for everyone.

**How do I see the tutorial again?**
Settings → How to play → Review the tutorial. This does not touch your progress.

**Does Reset progress erase everything?**
It clears your completed levels, your best Endless score and the tutorial, so the
game starts over exactly as it did on the first launch. Your language, theme and
haptics settings are kept.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. Never, for anything. The game works in aeroplane mode and will keep working
regardless of what happens to any server.

**Is there a subscription, or anything to buy inside the app?**
No. You bought the game once. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no timers
and nothing to unlock.

**Accessibility.**
VoiceOver labelling throughout, tap-to-place instead of dragging, Dynamic Type,
and dark and light themes. Haptics can be switched off in Settings.
