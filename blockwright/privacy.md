**Last updated: 27 September 2026. Applies to Blockwright version 1.0.0 and later.**

## The short version

Blockwright collects nothing, sends nothing, and contains no network code.

## What data the app collects

None.

Blockwright has no account, no sign-in, and never asks for an email address, a
name, or any other personal detail. It contains no analytics, no crash
reporting, no advertising identifier, and no third-party SDK of any kind. It
requests no iOS permissions — not the camera, the microphone, contacts, photos,
location, notifications, or the network.

## What is stored on your device

A single small file, private to the app, holding:

- which levels you have completed;
- your best Endless score;
- your chosen language, theme, and whether haptics are on;
- whether you have seen the first-run tutorial.

This file never leaves your iPhone. It is not backed up to any server operated
by the developer. If iCloud Backup is switched on for your device, Apple may
include it in your own encrypted device backup, under Apple's terms and entirely
under your control. Deleting Blockwright deletes the file.

You can erase the contents at any time from **Settings → Reset progress** inside
the app.

## Data sharing

None. There is nothing to share and no one to share it with. No data is sold,
rented, or disclosed to third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers.

## Children

Blockwright is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because it collects no data at
all, it collects no data from children. It contains no advertising, no purchases,
no links out of the app, and no way to communicate with anyone.

## Your rights

Because no personal data is collected or held by the developer, there is nothing
to request access to, correct, export, or have deleted. Under GDPR and CCPA
terms, the developer holds no personal data concerning you.

## Changes

If a future version of Blockwright ever collects anything, this policy will be
updated before that version ships and the change will be described in the
release notes. The intention is that it never will.
